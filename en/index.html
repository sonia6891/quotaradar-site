What is QuotaRadar?

It keeps an eye on your AI limits, so you stop checking web pages and never get cut off mid-conversation.

  1. Sign in as usualOpen each AI’s own sign-in page inside the app. No new account.
  2. Your limits show upWhat’s left on each AI and when it resets, on one screen.
  3. Know before you run outIt works out when you’ll hit the limit and tells you when to switch.

Everything you need, nothing you don’t

At a glance

Radar rings show what’s left on each AI and when it resets. Best now tells you which one to use. Several accounts per AI.

Know before you run out

Pace forecast tells you when you’ll hit the limit. Alerts at 20% and 5% left (you choose), plus reset and outage alerts.

Without opening the app

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the Dynamic Island count down to the reset.

Three looks

Radar rings, Gradient or Battery. Widgets and the Dynamic Island change together.

7-day stats

How much each AI used in the last 7 days, your busiest hours and your daily average.

Sign-ins stay on your phone

You sign in on each AI’s own page. Nothing goes through a server, and QuotaRadar never sees your password.

Apple Watch and Siri

Watch app and complications. Ask “How much is left in QuotaRadar” and Siri reads it out.
