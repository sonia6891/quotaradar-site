繁體中文

額度雷達（以下稱「本 App」）讓你在手機上查看 Claude、ChatGPT、Grok 等 AI 聊天服務的剩餘額度與重置時間。我們把 App 設計成不需要把你的資料交給我們：你的登入資料、AI 帳號與用量讀數都不會經過我們的伺服器，開發者收不到、也看不到你的任何個人資料。開發者只會收到下方第 7 節所說的匿名統計。

1. 不需要註冊帳號

本 App 沒有自己的會員系統，不會要求你提供姓名、電子郵件、電話或任何聯絡資料。

2. 登入資料只留在你的手機

  • 連結某個 AI 時，本 App 會在 App 內打開該服務自己的官方登入頁面，你的帳號密碼是直接輸入到該服務的網站，本 App 不會讀取或儲存你的密碼。
  • 登入成功後，該服務的登入 Cookie 會保存在手機系統內建的網頁元件（iOS 的 WKWebView／Android 的 WebView）中，只會傳送給該 AI 服務自己的網站，用來讀取你的用量頁面。
  • 你可以隨時在 App 裡按「中斷連結」，或刪除本 App，來清除這些登入資料。

3. 用量資料存在手機本機

本 App 讀到的剩餘額度百分比、重置時間、方案名稱，以及用來產生 7 天統計的歷史讀數，都只儲存在你的手機上（包含桌面小工具與動態島／即時動態所需的共用儲存空間），不會上傳到任何伺服器。刪除本 App 時這些資料會一起刪除。

4. 服務狀態查詢

為了提供「服務當機通知」，本 App 會向公開的服務狀態頁面發出匿名請求，例如 status.claude.com、status.openai.com。這些請求不帶有你的登入資料或任何可識別你的資訊；這些網站和所有網站一樣，可能會看到你的 IP 位址，並依其自身的隱私權政策處理。

5. 通知只在手機本機產生

快用完、額度恢復、服務當機等提醒，都是由手機本機排程與發送的本地通知，本 App 不使用推播伺服器，也不收集推播權杖。

6. 廣告成效衡量（Meta）

我們在 Facebook、Instagram 投放「下載本 App」的廣告。為了知道這些廣告帶來多少次下載，本 App 內建 Meta（Facebook）的 App 事件工具：當你安裝、打開本 App 或購買完整版時，會把這些事件，連同裝置型號、系統版本、App 版本等技術資訊傳給 Meta，用來計算廣告成效。

  • Android：為了讓 Meta 知道是哪一則廣告帶來安裝與購買，事件會附上 Android 的廣告 ID。你可以隨時在手機的「設定 → Google → 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID，刪除後就不會再附上。
  • iPhone：本 App 不會跳出「允許追蹤」的詢問視窗，因此不會取得 iPhone 的廣告識別碼（IDFA）。
  • 你的 AI 帳號、登入資料、用量讀數，絕不會傳給 Meta 或任何人。
  • Meta 依其自身的隱私權政策處理這些資料。

除此之外，本 App 沒有其他廣告工具，App 內不顯示廣告，也不會出售任何資料。

7. 匿名使用統計

為了知道 App 好不好用、有沒有出問題，本 App 會把幾種匿名事件傳到開發者自己的統計後台（架設在 Supabase）：

  • 第一次開啟本 App、購買完整版（商店交易編號、價格與幣別）、完整版被退款或撤銷。
  • 偵測到 AI 服務當機（哪一家服務、狀態頁顯示的嚴重程度）。
  • App 閃退（錯誤訊息的第一行），在下次打開 App 時送出。

每個事件只附上：App 隨機產生的安裝編號（與你的 Apple ID、Google 帳號、裝置序號都無關，刪除 App 就會消失）、iOS 或 Android、手機的地區設定（例如 TW）與 App 版本。不包含你的姓名、Email、AI 帳號、登入資料、用量讀數或廣告識別碼，也不會用來追蹤或辨識你。

8. 付款

本 App 可免費使用，另有一次買斷的「完整版」App 內購買（非訂閱）。付款完全由 Apple（App Store）或 Google（Google Play）處理，本 App 與開發者都拿不到你的付款資訊；App 只會向商店確認你是否已購買完整版。

9. 第三方 AI 服務

你在 App 內登入及使用的 Claude、ChatGPT、Grok、Gemini 等服務，由各自的公司提供，適用該公司的服務條款與隱私權政策。本 App 為獨立開發的第三方工具，與 Anthropic、OpenAI、xAI、Google、Meta 均無關聯。

10. 兒童隱私

English

QuotaRadar ("the app") shows how much of your Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and other AI chat limits you have left and when they reset. It is built so that you never hand your data to us: your sign-in, AI accounts and usage readings never go through a server of ours, and the developer does not receive or see any of your personal data. The developer only receives the anonymous statistics described in section 7.

1. No account

The app has no sign-up of its own. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number or other contact details.

2. Your sign-in stays on your phone

  • When you connect an AI service, the app opens that service's own sign-in page inside the app. You type your username and password into the service's own website; the app does not read or store your password.
  • After you sign in, the service's sign-in cookies are kept in your phone's system web view (WKWebView on iOS, WebView on Android) and are only sent to that AI service's own website, to read your usage page.
  • You can remove them at any time with "Disconnect" in the app, or by deleting the app.

3. Usage readings are stored on your device

The remaining percentages, reset times, plan names and the history used for 7-day stats are stored only on your phone (including the shared storage the widgets and the Dynamic Island / Live Activity need). They are never uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you delete the app.

4. Service status checks

For outage alerts the app sends anonymous requests to public status pages such as status.claude.com and status.openai.com. These requests carry no sign-in data and nothing that identifies you. Like any website, those pages may see your IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies.

5. Local notifications only

Running-low, reset and outage alerts are scheduled and shown by your phone itself. The app uses no push server and collects no push tokens.

6. Ad measurement (Meta)

We run "install this app" ads on Facebook and Instagram. To learn how many installs those ads bring, the app includes Meta's (Facebook's) App Events SDK: when you install or open the app, or buy the full version, that event is sent to Meta together with technical details such as device model, OS version and app version, to measure ad results.

  • Android: so Meta can tell which ad led to an install or purchase, the events include the Android advertising ID. You can reset or delete it any time in Settings → Google → Ads; once deleted it is no longer sent.
  • iPhone: the app never shows the "Allow tracking" prompt, so it never gets the iPhone advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Your AI accounts, sign-in data and usage readings are never sent to Meta or anyone else.
  • Meta handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Apart from this, the app contains no other advertising tools, shows no ads, and does not sell any data.

7. Anonymous usage statistics

To learn whether the app works well, the app sends a few anonymous events to the developer's own statistics service (hosted on Supabase):

  • The app opened for the first time; the full version bought (store transaction ID, price and currency); the full version refunded or revoked.
  • An AI service outage detected (which service, and the severity its status page shows).
  • An app crash (the first line of the error), sent the next time you open the app.

Each event carries only a random install ID made by the app (not linked to your Apple ID, Google account or device serial; it is gone when you delete the app), iOS or Android, your phone's region setting (for example TW) and the app version. It never includes your name, email, AI accounts, sign-in data, usage readings or advertising identifiers, and is not used to track or identify you.

8. Payments

The app is free to use, with a one-time "Full Version" in-app purchase (not a subscription). Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); neither the app nor the developer receives your payment details. The app only asks the store whether you own the full version.

9. Third-party AI services

Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini and the other services you sign in to are provided by their own companies under their own terms and privacy policies. QuotaRadar is an independent app and is not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Google or Meta.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
